    Questions

    What buyers and suppliers usually ask

    Anything else, ask in your enquiry and we’ll answer directly.

    Do you publish prices?

    No. Rates in this market move daily and depend on grade, quantity, route and payment terms, so we quote for each enquiry.

    Do you quote ex-plant or delivered?

    Usually delivered. Our price includes freight and delivery to your site (FOR destination). If you’d rather arrange your own truck, we can quote ex-plant, which covers the goods at the plant only.

    How are quality and specifications agreed?

    Before dispatch, both sides agree the grade and key parameters in writing. For meals that usually means protein, moisture, residual oil, fibre and sand and silica. For oils it usually means FFA, moisture and impurities, and colour. Test reports are shared where the contract calls for them.

    Do the goods pass through your warehouse?

    Usually not. Loads can travel straight from the plant to your site. Depending on the contract, Anvira either buys and resells the goods, or arranges the deal between buyer and supplier.

    What quantities do you work with?

    We work in metric tonnes (MT). Tell us the quantity per dispatch and in total, and we’ll tell you what’s practical for that commodity and route.

    Which documents come with a load?

    A GST tax invoice, an e-Way Bill where the rules require one, the lorry receipt and the weighment slip. Delivery is acknowledged at your site.
